Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains how the Dingdoo iPhone app (“Dingdoo”, “we”, “us”) handles information. If you have any questions, email dingdoo1124@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Dingdoo has no accounts and no sign-up.
  • Your alarms, reminders, tasks, plans and settings are stored on your iPhone. We do not run servers that receive them.
  • Motion data, microphone audio and speech are used only for the features you choose, and are not used for advertising.
  • Dingdoo shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device and usage information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Dingdoo does not ask to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Information stored on your device

Everything you create in Dingdoo, including alarms, wake-up missions, reminders, tasks, tags, plans and your preferences (language, appearance, accent color and time format), is saved in the app’s storage on your iPhone. It is not sent to us. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, Apple may include this data in your device backup according to your backup settings. Deleting an item in Dingdoo removes it from the app; deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Permissions and device features

Alarm (AlarmKit)

Dingdoo uses Apple’s AlarmKit so your alarms can ring at the time you set, even when the app is closed. Alarm times and labels are handed to iOS on your device only.

Notifications

Reminders and plan notifications are scheduled as local notifications on your iPhone. Dingdoo does not use a push-notification server.

Motion

Walking and squat missions read your iPhone’s motion sensors while the mission is on screen. Motion data is processed on your device, is not saved after the mission, and is not used for advertising or health research.

Camera

Some optional missions may use the front camera. When they do, images are analyzed in your device’s memory only, and are never saved, uploaded or used for advertising.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you use voice input to create a plan or reminder, Dingdoo uses the microphone and Apple’s speech recognition to turn your speech into text. Dingdoo asks for on-device recognition. Where iOS needs to use Apple’s speech service, Apple handles that audio under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Dingdoo does not keep the audio and does not use it for advertising.

Advertising

Dingdoo is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Right now, an ad may appear after you finish turning off an alarm, and you can close it. To deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google’s software in the app may collect information such as:

  • device and app information (for example device model, OS version and an app-specific identifier),
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate a general (not precise) location,
  • ad interactions (for example whether an ad was shown or tapped), and
  • diagnostic information about ad performance and crashes in the ads software.

Dingdoo does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so it does not give Google access to your device’s advertising identifier. Ads in Dingdoo are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Google’s use of this information is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy; you can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

If you email us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to improve Dingdoo, and you can ask us to delete it.

Children

Dingdoo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off any permission at any time in iOS Settings › Dingdoo. Features that need it will tell you what is missing.
  • You can reset your device’s advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have rights to know, delete or correct personal information. Because Dingdoo keeps your content on your device, you can delete it yourself at any time. For anything else, email us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Dingdoo handles information, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.

隐私政策

最后更新：October 6, 2026

本政策说明 iPhone App「叮嘟」（Dingdoo，以下称“我们”）如何处理信息。如有任何问题，请发邮件至 dingdoo1124@gmail.com。

简要说明

  • 叮嘟没有账号，也不需要注册。
  • 你的闹钟、提醒、任务、计划和设置都保存在你的 iPhone 上，我们没有接收这些内容的服务器。
  • 运动数据、麦克风声音和语音只用于你选择使用的功能，不用于广告。
  • 叮嘟展示 Google AdMob 广告。Google 可能会收集部分设备和使用信息来展示和衡量广告，详见下文。
  • 叮嘟不会请求跨其他公司的 App 和网站追踪你。

保存在你设备上的信息

你在叮嘟里创建的一切，包括闹钟、起床任务、提醒、任务、标签、计划，以及偏好设置（语言、外观、强调色、时间制式），都保存在 iPhone 上的 App 存储中，不会发送给我们。如果你使用 iCloud 云备份或电脑备份，Apple 可能会按你的备份设置把这些数据包含在设备备份里。在叮嘟里删除某一项即从 App 中移除；删除 App 会移除它在 iPhone 上的全部数据。

权限与设备功能

闹钟（AlarmKit）

叮嘟使用 Apple 的 AlarmKit，让闹钟在 App 未打开时也能按时响起。闹钟时间和名称只交给你设备上的 iOS。

通知

提醒和计划通知以本地通知的方式安排在你的 iPhone 上，叮嘟不使用推送服务器。

运动与健身

走路和深蹲任务在任务进行时读取 iPhone 的运动传感器。运动数据在设备上处理，任务结束后不保存，不用于广告或健康研究。

摄像头

部分可选任务可能使用前置摄像头。使用时，图像仅在设备内存中分析，不保存、不上传、不用于广告。

麦克风与语音识别

如果你用语音创建计划或提醒，叮嘟会使用麦克风和 Apple 的语音识别把语音转成文字。叮嘟会请求在设备上识别；当 iOS 需要使用 Apple 的语音服务时，相关音频由 Apple 依照 Apple 隐私政策 处理。叮嘟不保存音频，也不用于广告。

广告

叮嘟免费使用，由 Google AdMob 广告支持。目前，你关掉闹钟后可能会出现一条广告，可以关闭。为了投放、限制和衡量广告以及防止作弊，App 中的 Google 软件可能会收集以下信息：

  • 设备与 App 信息（例如设备型号、系统版本和 App 专属标识符）；
  • IP 地址，可能用于推测大致（非精确）位置；
  • 广告互动（例如广告是否展示或被点按）；
  • 广告软件的性能与崩溃诊断信息。

叮嘟不会请求「App 跟踪透明度」授权，因此不会让 Google 获取你设备的广告标识符。叮嘟中的广告仅限适合所有年龄的内容。Google 对这些信息的使用受 Google 隐私权政策 约束，详见 Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息。

我们不会做的事

  • 我们不出售你的个人信息。
  • 我们不使用第三方统计或崩溃收集服务。如果你在 iOS 设置中选择与 App 开发者共享分析数据，Apple 可能会向我们提供匿名的崩溃报告。
  • 我们不收集你的姓名、邮箱、通讯录、照片或精确位置。

如果你给我们发邮件

如果你联系支持，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容，仅用于回复你和改进叮嘟。你可以要求我们删除。

儿童

叮嘟不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

你的选择与权利

  • 你可以随时在 iOS 设置 › 叮嘟 中关闭任何权限，需要该权限的功能会告诉你缺少什么。
  • 你可以在 iOS 设置 › 隐私与安全性 中重置广告标识符或限制广告追踪。
  • 根据你所在地区（例如加利福尼亚州），你可能有权了解、删除或更正个人信息。由于叮嘟把你的内容保存在你的设备上，你可以随时自行删除；其他事项请发邮件给我们。

政策变更

如果叮嘟处理信息的方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面和上方日期；重大变化也会写在 App 的更新说明中。

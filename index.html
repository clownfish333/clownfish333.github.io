Wake up on time.
Keep track of the rest.

A wake-up alarm with missions, reminders that ring when it matters, and a simple daily planner. For iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store
Free · iPhone · iOS 26 or later · English and Simplified Chinese
⏰

Real system alarms

Built on Apple’s AlarmKit, so alarms ring at the time you set, even when the app is closed or your phone is locked.

🧩

Wake-up missions

Solve math, find the color block, repeat a memory sequence, type a phrase, walk or do squats before the alarm turns off.

✅

Wake-Up Check

A few minutes after you stop the alarm, Dingdoo checks that you’re still up. No answer? It rings again.

🔔

Reminders that escalate

For things you can’t miss, a reminder can ring like an alarm if you don’t confirm it in time.

🗓️

A simple daily plan

Keep a task list, drag tasks onto your day, and see today’s plan right on the Home tab.

🎨

Made to feel yours

Light or dark, seven accent colors or one of your own, 12- or 24-hour time, and a greeting that changes with the time of day.

Free, with one ad you can close

Dingdoo is free. After you finish turning off an alarm, you may see one ad, which you can close. There are no accounts and no sign-up.

叮嘟

叮嘟帮你按时起床，也帮你记住每件事。基于 Apple 系统闹钟（AlarmKit），锁屏和退出 App 时也能按时响铃；可以给闹钟加上数学题、颜色方块、记忆序列、输入短语、走路、深蹲等起床任务；开启确认起床后，几分钟后会再确认一次你醒了没有。提醒可以在未确认时升级为响铃；计划页可以记录任务、安排日程。免费使用，关闭闹钟后可能出现一条可以关掉的广告。

Common questions

How do I make sure my alarm will ring?

Open the Alarm tab and use Test Alarm. A real alarm rings in 10 seconds. Lock your phone and wait for it. If it rings, your alarms are set up correctly.

My alarm didn’t ring. What should I check?

In Dingdoo, go to Settings › Permissions and make sure System Alarm is allowed. You can also check iOS Settings › Dingdoo › Alarms & Timers. Your iPhone must be turned on; alarms can’t ring on a phone that is powered off.

Will alarms ring in Silent mode or a Focus?

Yes. Dingdoo uses Apple’s system alarms (AlarmKit), which ring even in Silent mode and during a Focus, whether the phone is locked or unlocked.

Do I have to unlock my phone to turn off an alarm with missions?

Yes. When an alarm with missions goes off, tap Start Missions. Dingdoo keeps ringing until you unlock your iPhone, open the app and finish your missions. You can turn this off in Settings › Background Guard.

What is Wake-Up Check?

When it’s on, Dingdoo checks in a few minutes after you stop the alarm. If you don’t respond, the alarm rings again so you don’t fall back asleep.

Why do I see an ad?

Dingdoo is free and supported by ads. You may see one ad after you finish turning off an alarm, and you can close it. Ads never appear while an alarm is ringing.

How do I change the language, appearance or accent color?

Go to Settings › General. Changes apply right away.

Where are the tutorials?

Settings › Tutorials has short guides for Alarm, Reminders and Plan.

How do I delete my data?

Your data stays on your iPhone. Delete individual alarms, reminders or tasks in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

常见问题

怎样确认闹钟能按时响？

在闹钟页使用「测试闹钟」，10 秒后会响一次真正的闹钟。锁上屏幕等它响起，响了就说明设置正确。

闹钟没有响，应该检查什么？

在叮嘟的 设置 › 权限 中确认「系统闹钟」已允许，也可以在 iOS 设置 › 叮嘟 › 闹钟与计时器 中查看。iPhone 需要保持开机，关机状态下闹钟无法响起。

静音模式或专注模式下会响吗？

会。叮嘟使用 Apple 系统闹钟（AlarmKit），在静音模式和专注模式下都会响，锁屏和解锁时都一样。

关掉带任务的闹钟需要解锁吗？

需要。起床任务在 App 里完成，所以需要解锁 iPhone 后完成任务——这正是帮你真正起床的关键。

什么是确认起床？

开启后，关掉闹钟几分钟后叮嘟会再问你一次是否醒着；没有回应就会再次响铃，避免睡回笼觉。

为什么会看到广告？

叮嘟免费使用，由广告支持。关掉闹钟后可能出现一条广告，可以关闭。闹钟响铃时不会出现广告。

怎样修改语言、外观或强调色？

设置 › 通用，修改立即生效。

教程在哪里？

设置 › 教程 里有闹钟、提醒、计划三份简短教程。

怎样删除我的数据？

你的数据保存在 iPhone 上。可以在 App 里逐条删除闹钟、提醒或任务，或删除 App 移除全部数据。
